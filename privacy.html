This Privacy Policy explains what information LSV ("we", "us") collects when you use the LSV obfuscator, website, dashboard, CLI and API (together, the "Service"), and how we use it. Last updated: November 4, 2026.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information — email address, and billing details when you subscribe to a paid plan.
  • API & license keys — the keys we issue you, and metadata about their use (request counts, timestamps, HWID lock status) so we can enforce plan limits.
  • Scripts you submit for obfuscation — see Section 2, this is handled differently from everything else.
  • Usage & log data — IP address, browser/CLI version, timestamps, and error logs, kept for security and abuse prevention.
  • Local preferences — your theme and accent color choice, stored only in your browser's localStorage, never sent to us.

2. How we handle the scripts you obfuscate

Scripts you submit — via the CLI, API, or the Dashboard uploader — are processed in memory for the duration of the build and are not written to persistent storage, logged, used to train any model, or shared with third parties. Once your protected output is returned to you, the input and intermediate build artifacts are discarded.

3. How we use information

  • To provide, maintain, and secure the Service;
  • To process payments and send billing-related notices;
  • To send service updates you can't opt out of (e.g. security notices) and, if you opt in, product updates;
  • To detect, prevent, and investigate abuse, fraud, or violations of our Terms of Service.

4. Data retention

We keep account data for as long as your account is active, plus a limited period afterward for legal and accounting purposes. Usage logs are kept for up to 90 days.

5. Third-party services

We rely on a small number of service providers to run LSV — for example, a payment processor to handle billing and an infrastructure provider to host the Service. We only share the minimum information each provider needs to do its job, and we don't sell your data.

6. Cookies & local storage

The website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. We use localStorage only to remember your appearance preferences (theme and accent color) on your own device.

7. Your rights

You can request a copy of your account data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account at any time by contacting us (see Section 9). Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws like the GDPR or CCPA.

8. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Changes to this policy & contact

We may update this policy from time to time; material changes will be posted here with an updated date. Questions? Contact us at privacy@example.com.
